MAIN PLAYER CHARACTER MODEL

Place the custom Mixamo character GLB here:
assets/character/character_main.glb

The Character Creator and the whole player/clone/multiplayer Hero system load that rig.
The model can also be placed in assets/character under its original uploaded name.
Godot must import the GLB before running the game.
Do not place this model in assets/naruto; that folder is no longer used by Hero.
