SHINOBI CLASH - PROGRESSION UPDATE

- Persistent shinobi level and XP. Normal enemy: 100 XP; boss: 500 XP.
- Every level grants 3 stat points. Every 10 levels grants 1 element spin.
- Stats tab lets you allocate points: Health (+5 max HP), Chakra (+5 max Chakra),
  MD (+5% faster MD charge), Stamina (+5 max stamina).
- Equipped bloodline gains mastery XP from kills (25 per enemy, 125 per boss).
- Bloodline mastery is tracked separately per bloodline, up to level 1000.
  The Bloodline tab shows mastery gates at levels 100, 500 and 1000.
- Element spins can roll Fire, Earth, Lightning, Water or Wind and save ownership/equipped element.
- Progress is saved in user://save.json.

NOTE: This update adds the element collection/spin and mastery unlock progression.
The project currently only contains the Fire Style jutsu set, and each existing
bloodline currently has one implemented active ability. The level 500 / 1000
power entries are progression unlock gates/placeholders; they do not create new
combat moves until those additional moves are authored.
